## Privacy Highlights

- **On-Device Mode:** Audio processed 100% locally using LiteRT on-device AI models. Zero audio leaves your device.
- **Cloud Mode:** Audio processed in ephemeral, isolated GPU workers. Uploaded files purged within 24 hours; stem outputs expire and are purged within 48 hours.
- **No AI Training:** Audio files and extracted stems are never used to train public or proprietary AI models.
- **GDPR & UMP Consent:** Integrated with Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) adhering to IAB TCF v2.2 with in-app consent revocation.
- **Account Deletion:** Clear self-service and email deletion paths complying with Google Play Data Safety policies.
