01 Overview

AI Vocal Remover ("the App", "we", "our") is developed and operated by Appvexis. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how it is processed, and how your privacy rights are safeguarded when using the application.

We respect your creative work and personal information. Our architecture is built around privacy-by-design: on-device separation keeps your media on your phone without touching any server, while cloud processing handles files strictly in temporary, isolated sandboxes and purges them according to a deterministic schedule.

02 Audio Processing Architecture

The App provides two distinct processing modes with fundamentally different privacy models:

1. On-Device Mode (100% Local & Private)

When you select 2-stem, 4-stem, or 5-stem offline separation, inference runs entirely on your device using LiteRT (TensorFlow Lite) models accelerated by your device's GPU or CPU.

  • Zero audio upload: Your selected audio or video file never leaves your device.
  • No account required: You can process tracks, export stems, and manage offline results without signing in.
  • Auxiliary analysis: BPM detection, musical key detection, and pitch/tempo shifting operate 100% locally on your device with no network transmission.
2. Studio Cloud Mode (Ephemeral Server Processing)

When you explicitly opt into Cloud processing for high-fidelity separation models:

  • Encrypted transit: Media is uploaded exclusively over HTTPS/TLS connections to our private storage.
  • Isolated computation: Cloud separation executes in ephemeral, sandboxed GPU worker environments.
  • Input deletion: Uploaded source audio is automatically deleted within 24 hours of upload/completion.
  • Result download window: Separated stem artifacts are retained in private encrypted cloud storage for up to 48 hours so you can listen, preview, and download them to your device, after which they are permanently deleted by automated cleanup routines.
Our Strict Guarantee: No AI Model Training

We never use your audio files or extracted stems to train, fine-tune, or benchmark public or proprietary artificial intelligence models. Your media is processed exclusively to deliver the separation result you requested.

B. Entitlements & Billing Data

In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled directly by Google Play. We do not receive, store, or process your credit card, debit card, or banking details. We record:

  • Google Play purchase tokens and order IDs to verify entitlements with the Google Play Developer API and prevent fraud or replay attacks.
  • Cloud seconds balance and ledger entries tracking credited processing seconds from active subscriptions.

C. Technical & Diagnostic Data

  • Cryptographic hash (SHA-256): Generated locally from audio files to check if identical tracks were already processed (memoized preflight), preventing unnecessary duplicate uploads.
  • Sanitized error codes: High-level error statuses (e.g., unsupported format, network timeout) are logged to diagnose job failures without collecting audio waveforms or stack traces containing personal data.
  • Rate-limiting identifiers: Public API requests use a pseudonymized hash to prevent abuse; raw IP addresses are not stored in our database.
Data We Never Collect

We do not collect physical location data, phone contacts, address books, biometric data, camera images, or microphone recordings. We do not use continuous background behavioral tracking SDKs.

06 Advertising & UMP Consent Choices

To support free features, the App integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). We implement the official Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK, which complies with the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2):

  • Consent before initialization: In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland, ad measurement and SDK initialization are paused until you specify your consent preferences.
  • Personalized vs. Non-personalized: If you decline consent for personalized advertising, Google AdMob serves non-personalized ads using contextual signals only.
  • Ad-free options: Users who purchase the "Offline Ad-Free Lifetime" upgrade have rewarded ads removed from offline workflows. Active paid cloud subscribers receive an ad-free experience while entitled.
  • Changing your choices anytime: You have the right to revisit and update your consent choices at any time. Open the in-app Settings > Ad privacy choices to adjust your advertising preferences.

07 Storage, Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard cryptographic and operational safeguards to protect your data:

  • Android Keystore encryption: Local session tokens are encrypted using AES-256-GCM via Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences.
  • TLS encryption: All data in transit between the client, Cloudflare API, and GPU compute is protected with modern TLS encryption.
  • Access control: Database access is strictly authenticated; users can only query, stream, or delete their own job records and artifacts.
  • Deterministic cleanup schedule:
    • Cloud input audio: Automatically purged within 24 hours.
    • Cloud output stems: Automatically purged within 48 hours.
    • Account metadata: Maintained until you request account deletion.
